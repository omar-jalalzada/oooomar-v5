Research drawn from Meggs' History of Graphic Design, museum collections (V&A, MoMA, Cooper Hewitt), Wikipedia and Wikidata, checked across nine regional and period studies. Where sources disagree on a date, the more widely cited one is used and the dispute is kept in the research notes.

Images of work come from Wikimedia Commons under their own licences, credited beside each one. Work still in copyright is shown as a small thumbnail for study and commentary, marked fair use, and links to its source.
