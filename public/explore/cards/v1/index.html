Exploring Cards

Five structurally different directions for content cards — each showing writing, lab, and work. Not variations of one layout: genuinely different spatial ideas. v1 is range-finding before we go deep.

The question: what card structure is scannable in under two seconds, feels ownable to this site, and works across three content types without forcing them into the same mold?
