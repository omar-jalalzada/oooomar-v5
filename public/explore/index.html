The making of this site.

A public log of the iteration behind omar.build — the sketches, the dead-ends, and the directions worth keeping. Every version is preserved, never overwritten. This is the workshop floor; the Labs are the finished pieces.

v7 Unified cards + radial focus-wave reveal Every card is one component now — a media tile plus a title, with type and date revealed only on hover; just the footprint varies. The camera dollies back from a tight start and the work comes into focus in rings spreading outward from Omar — nearest first. 19 JUN 2026 → v6 A pannable canvas — Omar at the centre of a world The work lives in a space larger than the screen, organized around Omar at varied sizes — live previews for labs, excerpts for writing, type/date on hover. The entrance is a camera pull-back (no flying cards); drag or scroll to explore the rest. 19 JUN 2026 → v5 The work orbits Omar Omar holds the centre and the work settles into a constellation around the logotype; cursor parallax gives the field depth. One immersive screen — the first move away from the scroll-based layouts. 19 JUN 2026 → v4 Self-constructing wordmark + cleaner affordances Closed the hairline seams in the logotype, and changed the reveal to a left-to-right self-construction — each modular block grows into place. Dropped the SCROLL label; the work now peeks above the fold as the cue. Nav mark became the O, and "Work" was removed from the dock. 19 JUN 2026 → v3 OMAR logotype as the centerpiece Dropped the headline entirely. Omar's sketched wordmark — a bold, modular, constructed logotype in electric blue — rebuilt from his exact geometry and revealed in horizontal slices, with counter-dots that gently track the cursor. The dock is now pinned to the window bottom. Cards fill electric blue on hover, inverting to white. 19 JUN 2026 → v2 Per-letter reveal, floating dock, asymmetric work Type materializes letter by letter and kerns in; the italic word draws its own underline. ConstellationCONCEPT 02 · CONSTELLATION
v4 A logotype at the centre, simpler cards, calmer hover A bigger reset, driven by mockups. The hero identity card became a graphical centre: the OMAR logotype self-constructs, then the cards ripple in around it across a loose field. Cards are now near-square and deliberately plain — a placeholder visual block plus a title — and on hover they reveal a description and a meta line while the shadow grows into a soft, category-tinted glow. The busy back-pattern motifs are gone; backs are a plain solid surface seen only mid-flip. 21 JUN 2026 → v3 One typeface, hidden metadata, hover that echoes the back Tightened the deck toward restraint. Unified all cards on Hanken Grotesk — Instrument Serif now belongs to the hero alone, so the serif signals the protagonist. Fixed the spacing around the hero (the wider card had collapsed its gap to its neighbours) by packing columns from cumulative widths with one uniform gap, and gave columns varied widths to pair with the height variation. The type label and date are now hidden by default and fade in only on hover. And the hover flood now uses each card's own back motif behind a legibility scrim, so the deal and the hover speak one visual language. 19 JUN 2026 → v2 Organized board, a protagonist card, and a two-beat reveal Reworked the entrance and the layout after the spiral felt random and the simultaneous fly-and-flip felt busy. Cards now sit in tidy category-grouped columns flanking a larger hero identity card (alive by default, never flips). The reveal split into two clean beats: deal the cards in face-down — their backs colored by category, so the board shows organized color regions — then a calm flip wave turns them face-up. Card heights and templates vary (essay, pull-quote, lab, work-list, travel photo) to break the sameness. 19 JUN 2026 → v1 Everything is a card on an endless canvas A different bet from Stage: not a kinetic identity, but a kinetic space. Writing, labs, and about (bio, work, travel) are all cards arranged in a phyllotaxis spiral around a center identity card. One card lands cinematically, then the rest ripple outward — arriving face-down and flipping to minimal fronts. The field pans in any direction with momentum and soft rubber-band edges; hover floods each card with category color and life. 19 JUN 2026 →
