ConstellationCONCEPT 02 · CONSTELLATION
v4 The logotype at the center A bigger reset, driven by mockups. The OMAR logotype builds itself at the center. Cards ripple into a loose field around it. Each card is nearly square and deliberately plain, with a visual block and title. Hover reveals the description and metadata, while the shadow grows into a soft tinted glow. I removed the busy back patterns. 21 JUN 2026 → v3 One typeface, hidden metadata, quieter hover I pulled the deck toward restraint. Every card now uses Hanken Grotesk. Instrument Serif belongs to the hero, making it the clear protagonist. I fixed the collapsed spacing around the wider hero and varied the column widths. Type and date stay hidden until hover. Each hover uses the card’s back motif behind a soft scrim, tying both states together. 19 JUN 2026 → v2 An organized board with a two-beat reveal The spiral felt random. Flying and flipping at once felt busy. I grouped cards into columns around a larger hero card that stays alive and face up. The reveal now has two beats. Cards arrive face down in color groups, then turn over in a calm wave. Different heights and templates break the repetition. 19 JUN 2026 → v1 Everything is a card on an endless canvas A kinetic space built from cards. Writing, experiments, and About form a phyllotaxis spiral around an identity card. One card lands first. The rest ripple outward, arriving face down before flipping to simple fronts. The field pans in any direction with momentum and soft edges. Hover fills each card with its category color. 19 JUN 2026 →

Sacred GeometryCONCEPT 04 · DYNAMIC THUMBNAILS
v3 A cosmology of spinning worlds Each circle became a world spinning on its own tilted axis. Two crossing meridian rings show the rotation. Inner worlds move faster. Every axis has a different tilt. Front arcs stay bright while back arcs recede. The full lattice turns slowly as one structure. 22 JUN 2026 → v2 The lattice tilts, breathes, and turns I tilted the Flower of Life about 18° and set it into a slow spin. A sine wave moves outward from the center, pulsing each circle through the plane. Near circles feel brighter and heavier. Distant circles recede. A canvas transform keeps the ellipse projection accurate. 22 JUN 2026 → v1 Five geometric constructions I compared five constructions as static 16:9 thumbnails: Flower of Life, Interlocking Triangles, Golden Spiral, Compass and Straightedge, and Metatron’s Cube. A word determines each seed. Flower of Life became the direction to animate. 22 JUN 2026 →
