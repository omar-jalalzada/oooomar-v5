Taste is a cosmic-scale web of ideas, feelings, and references that makes something feel right to a human. It isn't downloaded; it's accrued — through intentional observation, repeated exposure, and the patience to notice *why* something works.

You can't shortcut it. You can only spend more time looking, and stay honest about what you actually see.
