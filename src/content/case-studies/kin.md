## Designing the company

I was Kin's co-founder, CEO and sole designer. My work covered the product, visual identity,
marketing site, pitch deck, fundraising materials and paid ads. I designed the iOS and Android
apps while leading the company from an early prototype through a public launch.

## Making habit-building social

Kin treated accountability as part of the product's foundation. People chose small habits,
completed them through a daily ritual, built streaks with others and shared progress with the
people supporting them.
