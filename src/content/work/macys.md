## My role

At Tigerspike, I reimagined key parts of the Macy's and Bloomingdale's iPhone apps, focusing on
people's personality and behavior to create contextual shopping experiences: a thematic home
screen, flatter category navigation, a product page led by imagery, and lists and a wedding
registry that adapt when you're in a store.

I found new opportunities in mobile retail through market research, user interviews, and
on-site experiments, then tested what we learned in stakeholder meetings and user testing
sessions. I designed the new features as high-fidelity prototypes, with detailed documentation
for the handoff. I worked alongside Pauly Ting, a fellow UX designer.

## Outcome

Macy's stakeholders received the strategy and prototypes very well, and we were engaged for the
next stage of the project. The work also inspired a range of new features on the product roadmap
for the next few releases.
