## My role

I joined Alto as the Lead Product Designer. At the time, it was relatively small: two product
designers, 20 engineers, no product managers, one location, and 100 employees. I started by
leading design for the patient app, helping patients manage their medications, save money on
refills, improve health outcomes, and avoid needing to contact us during deliveries.

As the company grew, we hired a Head of Design. I supported him with recruiting and product
strategy, growing the design team by hiring six product designers, two design managers, and two
UX researchers to support all of our product initiatives.

After my first year at Alto, we shifted our strategic focus from growth to profitability. I was
asked to lead design for the Internal Tools & Services team, decreasing the price per shipment
and automating operations to reduce manual labor. I led a team of three designers and two product
managers supporting four engineering teams.

I left Alto after 2.8 years. By then, the company had more than 1,000 employees, 13 locations,
over $550 million in funding, and had filled more than three million prescriptions.

## What is Alto?

Alto is a vertically integrated digital pharmacy. A doctor can view a patient's prescriptions
through the Provider Portal and send new ones to an Alto pharmacist. The pharmacist processes
the prescription using an internal tool called Wunderbar, and the fulfillment team uses the same
tool to package the medication and send a courier to deliver it. Patients manage their
prescriptions and deliveries in the mobile app.
