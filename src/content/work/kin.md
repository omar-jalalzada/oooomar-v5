## My role

As a co-founder, I started Kin with Justin Kan, who co-founded Twitch; Amitt Mahajan, who
co-created FarmVille; and Damien Kan, who co-founded Shogun. We initially worked together to
build an MVP and validate our hypothesis with real users. The beta was well received, so I
stepped in as CEO to launch Kin publicly.

As CEO, I raised a seed round and built our team. I hired three engineers, a product manager,
and a health advisor from Johns Hopkins to develop our product and coaching services. I managed
our advisors and investors, as well as marketing strategy through contractors supporting
direct-to-consumer and influencer partnerships.

As a product designer, I conducted more than 80 user-research interviews and designed the iOS
and Android apps. I also led product, design, and brand strategy and execution. I worked closely
with my CTO and product manager to create a quarterly roadmap with clear success metrics mapped
into two-week sprints, focusing us on the features most critical to users.

## What is Kin?

Kin was a social wellness app that helped people build healthy habits based on their goals and
provided a support system to hold them accountable.
