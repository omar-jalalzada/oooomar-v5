Assigning each letter a musical control created one system for visuals, interaction, and sound. The O controls drums. The M controls distortion. The A controls tempo. The R controls volume. Every gesture changes the image and sequencer together. Browser constraints shaped the final experience too. Sound begins through deliberate interaction, and the fallback quietly preserves the wordmark when WebGPU is unavailable.
