Overlap gives the point field its depth. Dense areas create flesh. Sparse areas become hair, atmosphere, or motion. A single batched path flattened those differences. Drawing each dot separately preserved the accumulation. Normalizing the underlying walk also separated density from scale, allowing more points to enrich a form without making it larger. The same system can now hold a jellyfish, a word, or an orbiting solar system.
