Every style we use today was someone's answer to the style before it. Swiss grids came out of the Bauhaus. The Bauhaus came out of Constructivism and De Stijl. Apple and Google took the whole lineage and stopped there. This is the map, from William Morris to Liquid Glass, across Europe, the Americas, the Middle East and East Asia. Scroll through time. Bars are movements, circles are people. Click anything to see its principles and what it led to. Next, one movement at a time, drawn as motion.
