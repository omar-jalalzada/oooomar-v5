# Omar — Identity & Ethos

> Baseline reference for the personal site (and anything downstream). Captures who Omar is,
> how he sees, and what he's loyal to aesthetically — so design directions, copy, and content
> stay rooted in him rather than in category clichés.
>
> **Provenance:** Omar's answers to six identity questions (June 2026), assembled with Notion AI
> from his journal. A mix of his own words (quoted verbatim where it matters — e.g. the manifesto)
> and synthesis. Treat the verbatim quotes as canon; treat the synthesis as a working read.

## 1) Origin & heritage

A **diaspora / "in-between worlds"** path rather than a single-place identity. It shows up in how
he talks about identity: non-dogmatic, self-authored, skeptical of default narratives.

- **Born / raised:** Kabul, Afghanistan (1988). Family fled as war refugees to Pakistan at age 5.
  Lived in Pakistan until 19, then moved to California, USA.
- **Heritage:** Afghan American.
- **Path:** Maker → founder/operator → design leader → advisor. Currently **Head of Design at
  Sublime Security**.
- **Why design / why build:** "The art of being creative — making something out of nothing. The
  joy of making things and bringing them into the world."
- **Homepage lead:** "A design lead who's in love with designing — **a craftsman first**."

## 2) Worldview / mental models

- **Systems lens.** Prefers designing the environment, constraints, and feedback loops so good
  work happens — rather than "managing people into performance."
- **Rational optimism + compassion + mindfulness**, with a consistent ethic of **"make no harm"**
  and continuous learning/relearning.
- **Critical thinking.** Questions structures, resists dogma, chooses what's true/useful over
  what's popular.
- **Leadership as midfielder.** A player-coach in craft (still making, still in the details), but
  conceptually a **midfielder** — connecting strategy to execution, translating across functions.

**Operating principles:**
- Doing it for the love of design.
- Design is for business outcomes: "I'm capitalist; the goal of design is to make more money for
  the business."
- Outcomes improve when talented people work together — the sum is greater than the parts.

**On taste:** "Like human consciousness — a cosmic scale of interconnected ideas/feelings/mood/
visuals that makes something feel really good to a human. It's earned and learned through
intentional observation of the world."

**Contrarian belief:** At startups, "good design" isn't automatically great aesthetics — it's
designing the best solution within constraints to deliver business outcomes.

## 3) Aesthetic loyalties

- **Design influences:** Bauhaus, Dieter Rams, Teenage Engineering, Apple design.
- **Art influences:** Impressionism (capturing the essence of what we find beautiful).
- **Cultural aesthetics:** Islamic tile work, miniature drawings.
- **Craft disciplines he practices:** photography, Arabic calligraphy, illustration/drawing —
  disciplines where composition, restraint, and repetition matter.
- **Taste tendencies:** precision + calm + texture (not flashy novelty); intentional, timeless,
  materially grounded.
- **Site vibe:** "A creative combination of styles — prioritizing clean minimalism with elements
  of maximalism." Portfolio must communicate good design; **writing and experiments carry equal
  weight** to the portfolio.

## 4) Voice & temperament

- Natural voice is **calm, direct, thoughtful** — "observations and principles," not "hot takes."
- **Non-performative.** Values-driven (mindfulness, compassion) without moralizing.
- **Tone target:** measured confidence, high signal, low ego, clear craft authority.
- **Avoid:** grandiose / self-fulfilling-prophecy language.
- **Tone:** human, approachable, friendly — professional with charm. "Humble, but confident;
  resolute; strong opinions loosely held."
- **POV:** first-person throughout ("I").

## 5) Productive tensions — the brand engine

- **Soft mind / sharp standards** — compassion + rigor.
- **Calm demeanor / high ambition** — not loud, not casual.
- **Maker + executive** — leads orgs and strategy, but still cares deeply about color/type/
  composition and the *actual artifact*.
- **Skeptical of dogma / committed to principles** — questions structures while living by a clear
  internal code.
- **Art + outcomes** — a photography/calligraphy sensibility applied to product/business
  constraints.

**Core tension (his words):** "I live in the middle after I define the extremes (e.g., we mean
nothing in the vast cosmic arena, yet we're made of atoms with cosmic-level complexity). I find
peace making a life of my own in the middle ground."

**Common misread to correct:** "People expect me to be troubled/sad as 'a kid from Kabul,' but I'm
confident and comfortable in my skin. I know my craft, and often surprise people with depth and
intentionality."

## 6) The obsession / craft tell

- **Detail fixation:** "No single detail is good or bad on its own — design is about how elements
  work in harmony within the full composition."
- **Signature in critiques:** depth of understanding complex systems, and distilling them into
  clear, easy-to-understand frameworks.
- **Motif:** "None — I'm very adaptable."
- Candidate craft tells (to confirm over time): typographic weight & spacing (kerning, leading,
  optical alignment); cropping & framing (photography); the gesture of a line (calligraphy); a
  bias toward restraint — fewer elements, more meaning per pixel.

## Design signals (synthesis — for briefs & directions)

Distilled from the above; a working read, not canon.

- **Lead identity:** *craftsman first* — a maker who happens to lead. The artifact matters.
- **Not the Kabul-trauma narrative.** Confident, intentional, surprising depth. Forward-looking.
- **Composition over ornament.** Bauhaus/Rams restraint as the floor; Islamic tilework / miniature
  / calligraphy as the source of warmth, pattern, and a non-Western texture that's authentically
  his — a way to be distinctive without cliché.
- **"Clean minimalism with elements of maximalism."** The productive design tension to explore:
  disciplined structure that breaks into richness somewhere intentional.
- **Equal weight:** writing + experiments are not secondary to portfolio.
- **Voice:** first-person, measured, low-ego, principle-led; charm over polish; no grandiosity.
- **Middle-ground philosophy:** define the extremes, then live between them — a possible
  structural/conceptual spine for a direction.
