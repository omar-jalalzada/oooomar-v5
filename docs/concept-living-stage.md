# Concept — The Living Stage

> The chosen direction for omar.build, derived from references Omar pulled (nural.news,
> opacity.com, interfacecraft.dev) — see `docs/omar.md` for identity. Prototyped in live code,
> not Paper, because the whole idea is **motion**.

## The brief

The site is **not a website with navigation and a content grid**. It's a **cinematic stage with a
single living centerpiece**: you arrive to white, something **materializes**, and it reveals the
work as **tactile, interactive things** you explore. Motion is the medium.

### Locked constraints
- **White only.** No dark mode. Black grounds are overplayed. Optimistic + futuristic.
- **Cinematic entrance → reveal.** A graphic/light moment materializes, then the work emerges
  from it — items appearing in sequence as interactive cards, and/or one graphic element that
  reveals and uses scroll to reach all content. (To experiment.)
- **Craft-first.** The work is the hero. Omar's bio lives on About. No grandiose statement.
- **Fonts must tell a story** — the typeface choice carries meaning, not neutral defaults.
- **One living centerpiece**, minimal chrome, near-monochrome white + one luminous accent.
- **Material depth + motion** — nothing flat; spring-physics motion (interface-craft ethos).

### Stolen moves (from the three references)
- nural — the page is an **instrument** you operate; monospace UI + one pop color; keycap affordances.
- interface craft — a **collection of tactile artifacts** arranged like treasures; texture + depth.
- opacity — a soft form **materializing from nothing** via motion; atmosphere; cinematic entrance.

### Type story (v1 — to test)
- **Instrument Serif** (display) — cinematic title-card serif; the human, editorial, art voice.
- **Monospace** (labels/UI) — the machine/precision/instrument layer.
- Together they tell Omar's core tension: *art × system, craftsman operating the machine.*

### Open / experimenting
- What the "one object" is: cards-in-sequence vs one graphic element that scroll-reveals content.
- Exact accent + the luminous-materialization treatment (aurora vs prism vs single hue).
